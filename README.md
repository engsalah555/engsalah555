### 🎓 Professional Profile & Engineering Philosophy

I am an academically grounded **Software Engineer** with a deep commitment to the craft of computer science and systems architecture. My engineering approach balances theoretical rigor—rooted in algorithmic efficiency, clean abstractions, and domain modeling—with practical, high-velocity software delivery.

### 💼 Production Projects & Architectural Case Studies

#### 1. 🚜 [Smart Farmer Ecosystem](https://github.com/engsalah555/smart-farmer) — *AI-Powered IoT AgriTech Platform*
*An end-to-end intelligent agricultural management system bridging computer vision, IoT telemetry, and e-commerce into a unified platform.*
* **Mobile Client ([Flutter / Dart](https://github.com/engsalah555/smart-farmer)):** Features an **on-device & cloud AI leaf disease diagnostic engine**, an interactive agronomy chatbot, real-time IoT soil and climatic telemetry displays, seasonal crop lifecycle trackers, and a digital farmer marketplace.
* **Backend Core ([Python](https://github.com/engsalah555/smart-farmer) & [Laravel REST API](https://github.com/engsalah555/smart-farmer-backend)):** Architected with a multi-layered RESTful service layer handling secure token authentication, agricultural metadata cataloging, automated event reporting, and Python-powered inference pipelines for agricultural analytics.
* **Key Competencies:** `Computer Vision Integration`, `IoT Telemetry Ingestion`, `Multi-Tier Architecture`, `State Management`.

#### 2. 📹 [Enterprise Surveillance & VMS System](https://github.com/engsalah555/monitoring_application) — *Video Management Software (VMS)*
*A mission-critical enterprise surveillance platform engineered to manage complex CCTV/NVR network topologies and live video streams across distributed physical sites.*
* **Clean Architecture & DDD:** Strict separation into distinct `Domain` (Entities, Use Cases, Value Objects), `Data` (Repositories, Data Sources), and `Presentation` layers.
* **Core Capabilities:** Hierarchical organizational tree management (Companies, Branches, Sectors), Network Video Recorder (NVR) device provisioning across multiple industrial brands, live multi-grid camera matrices (1x1 up to 16x16), and synchronized DVR timeline playback controls.
* **Key Competencies:** `Domain-Driven Design`, `Network Protocols & RTSP Streaming`, `State Machines`, `Complex UI Wizards`.

#### 3. ⛽ [Car Fuel Monitor](https://github.com/odaibishr/Car-Fuel-Monitor) — *Geospatial Fleet & Consumption Analytics*
*A collaborative mobile engineering project co-developed with an engineering peer, designed to provide drivers and fleet operators with precision fuel consumption analytics and routing optimization.*
* **Architecture:** Structured around a **Feature-First** architecture powered by **BLoC/Cubit** state management, repository pattern, and service locator dependency injection (`get_it`).
* **Geospatial & Navigation Engine:** Real-time GPS location tracking, proximity-based gas station discovery via OpenRouteService and Google Maps APIs, distance/fuel consumption estimators, and persistent user telemetry synced across **Supabase** and **Firebase**.
* **Key Competencies:** `Geospatial Calculations`, `BLoC Architecture`, `Dependency Injection`, `RTL/Arabic-First UI Systems`.
